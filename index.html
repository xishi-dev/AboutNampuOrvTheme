Chapter 1

Origin

Every world begins as a single image.

I build worlds. Not the one we live in, but the kind other people can walk into and not want to leave.

A subway station falling apart. A school where something walks the halls after the last bell. A floating market hiding a mystery under the water. Each one starts as a single image in my head, gets shaped in Blender, and comes alive in Roblox and Unity.

Some of them become videos instead, cut in After Effects so every frame lands on the beat.

If you are reading this sentence, this story now has one reader.
